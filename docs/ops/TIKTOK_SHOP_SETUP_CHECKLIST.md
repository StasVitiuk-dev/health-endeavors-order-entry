# TikTok Shop: what to have ready, and how long it takes (Nov 27, 2026)

Status: **PROPOSED** (owner question of Nov 27, night). A checklist only: no names, ID numbers, tax
numbers, bank details or addresses belong in this file or in chat. They go only into TikTok's own forms.

**Source caveat:** written from seller guides and trade news published in 2026, not from TikTok's own
policy pages (they did not come up in the search). TikTok changes these rules often. Before applying,
check the current lists in TikTok Shop **Seller Center** (its "Qualification Center" for categories).
The sources are listed at the end.

Related: `BUSINESS_EMAIL_PLAN.md` (which address the account uses), `BUILD_TRACKER_2_WEEKS.md`
("Waits on the owner", item 2: business address before the seller accounts), and the decision of
Nov 24 that **Shopify is the hub**: TikTok Shop is connected through Shopify.

## Whose account

- **OWNER DECISION (Nov 27, recorded as given):** the business is registered to the owner's partner; the
  owner runs the selling. The partner agrees to the shop being opened for the business.
- **Recommended: a business account in the business's name**, not a personal account. A personal account
  would use one person's own tax number, so the sales and income would be reported as that person's, not the
  business's, and would not match Shopify and the business bank account.
- The partner is listed as **owner**: everyone who owns **25% or more** must be listed, with ID. The person who
  runs the shop is listed as the **primary representative**.
- Have a **letter of authorization** ready: a short letter signed for the business saying the representative may
  run the shop. TikTok may ask for it.
- Afterwards each person uses their own login (Seller Center can add users). One shared password is avoided.

## What to have ready

Everything must be spelled **exactly the same everywhere** (IRS letter, state registration, bank, TikTok form),
or verification fails.

**The business**
1. Legal business name and the official business address. Mail forwarding to the owner's state is a separate
   question; see the address note below.
2. The EIN, and the IRS letter that proves it: **CP 575**, or a **147C** from the IRS if the original is lost
   (a 147C can take a few days).
3. The state registration papers of the business.
4. A **business bank account** in the business's name, for payouts.

**The people**
5. The owner's (partner's) government ID: a driver's license or passport.
6. The representative's government ID.
7. The signed letter of authorization.

**Contact and shipping**
8. Email and phone, never a personal email (owner question, Nov 28). From `BUSINESS_EMAIL_PLAN.md`:
   - **Login email: `admin@`**: the address that owns the seller account, with the strongest 2-step login,
     never used for sending. If the shop is opened before the business email exists, use one new address made
     only for the business, then change it later in Seller Center.
   - **Customer contact: `support@`**, wherever the form asks how buyers reach the shop. Order and shipping
     notices go to `orders@` (an alias of the same mailbox).
   - **Phone:** the representative's own mobile is the practical choice for the sign-up and login codes; buyers
     are given the `support@` email, not this number. Some platforms refuse internet phone numbers (for
     example Google Voice) for codes; a real mobile line avoids that.
   - Why not a personal email: the shop belongs to the business. A personal inbox that is locked, closed or
     changed would lock the shop too, and the partner could not be given the account.
9. Ship-from and returns address: where the products physically leave from (it can differ from the official
   business address). **Privacy:** this address is printed on every package and on every customer's return
   label, so every buyer sees it. The owner will ship from home at first and wants returns at home (Nov 27).
   To keep the home address private, the usual choice is a **private mailbox near home** (for example at a
   UPS Store): a street address with a box number that accepts every carrier, used as the ship-from and returns
   address. A USPS PO Box takes USPS only, unless that post office offers "street addressing". OWNER DECISION
   REQUIRED: home address or a mailbox. The public seller information required by US law for high-volume
   marketplace sellers (the INFORM Consumers Act) shows the **business** address. A home address is shown
   there only when a seller has no business address, and then only the country and state.

**The products: beauty and personal care is a gated category.** The shop applies for approval before
listing. The products are body care and skincare, **not** supplements; dietary supplements would be
invite-only.

10. For each product: photos of **all sides of the package and label**.
11. The full ingredient list.
12. Batch and expiry details where they apply. Products more than three years old must not be sold.
13. **No medical claims** ("treats", "cures", and the like): such listings are blocked.
14. **Ask the lab** whether the FDA cosmetics registration and product listing (the 2022 MoCRA law) is done.
    TikTok may ask about it.
15. The **product list from the lab** (launch item R8): the same list the whole launch waits on.

## How long

| Step | About |
|---|---|
| Gathering the documents | 1–3 days if they exist (a 147C letter can add a few days) |
| Sign-up and identity check | 1–3 days, sometimes up to a week |
| Beauty category approval | a few days to about a week |
| Connecting through Shopify, listing products | 1–2 days once the product list is ready |
| **Total** | **about 1–2 weeks**, faster when every document matches |

## Address note (owner question, Nov 27)

The official business address is in another state from where the owner lives. Recommended: keep the
official address and have the mail **scanned and forwarded**. Either through the registered agent or
virtual-office service at that address (if that is what it is), or through a virtual mailbox with a street
address in that state. The ship-from and returns address can be the owner's own (or a PO box nearby).
OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: what the official address is (a registered agent, someone's home, or owned
property), so the exact forwarding steps can be given.

**Ask an accountant or a registration service** whether a business registered in one state but run and
shipped from another must also register in the second state ("foreign registration"), and where sales tax
applies. Not confirmed here.
