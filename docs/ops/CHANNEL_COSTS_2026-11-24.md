# What each sales channel costs (looked up Nov 24, 2026)

Status: CURRENT as a planning note. Every figure below was read from public pages or
articles on Nov 24, 2026 and must be **checked on the platform's own page before signing
up**: platforms change fees, and two sources below disagree. United States figures.

Owner decision this supports: **Shopify is the hub** for Amazon, Etsy and TikTok Shop
(DECIDED Nov 24: the owner agreed after asking for an opinion; selling out fast is fine,
selling more than is in stock is not, and one shared stock count is what prevents it).

## Connecting the channels to Shopify

| Channel | How it connects | Cost of the connection |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon | Shopify Marketplace Connect (Shopify's own app; also Walmart, eBay, Target Plus) | Free to install. First 50 marketplace orders a month free; after that 1% of each order, capped at $99 a month. |
| TikTok Shop | TikTok's app for Shopify | Not confirmed in this lookup (believed free to install). Check the app page. |
| Etsy | **OPEN.** Shopify's help page still lists Etsy in Marketplace Connect, but the English app listing no longer does and a Shopify support post says Etsy is being retired there for new connections. | Either a paid third-party connector app, or keep Etsy separate and bring its orders and money report into the dashboard by file (the money report reader is built, PR #52). OWNER DECISION REQUIRED once the Etsy shop exists. |

## What each channel takes from a sale

| Channel | Fixed cost | Per sale |
|---|---|---|
| Shopify website | Shopify plan (monthly) | Card processing about 2.9% + $0.30 on the Basic plan (the estimate already used in Real profit) |
| Amazon | Professional plan $39.99 a month | Referral fee, Beauty / Health & Personal Care: 8% when the item is $10 or less, 15% above $10; $0.30 minimum |
| Etsy | **one-time $29 set-up fee** to open the shop (shown to the owner on Etsy's own sign-up page, Nov 23 evening; my first lookup missed it and said "none") | $0.20 per listing (renews every 4 months or on sale) + 6.5% transaction fee + 3% + $0.25 payment processing. Optional Offsite Ads: 12–15% only on sales that come from those ads |
| TikTok Shop | none | Referral fee 6% for most categories on TikTok's public table (May 2026); some seller sites report 8% since Aug 2026 (UNCONFIRMED, check Seller Center). New sellers: 3% for the first 30 days. Creator (affiliate) commission is extra and chosen by the seller |

Worked example, one $25 bottle, before postage and the product's own cost:

| Channel | Fees | Left |
|---|---|---|
| Shopify website | about $1.03 | $23.97 |
| TikTok Shop at 6% (8%) | $1.50 ($2.00) | $23.50 ($23.00) |
| Etsy | about $2.83 | $22.17 |
| Amazon | $3.75 | $21.25 (plus the $39.99 monthly plan) |

These are the same kind of figures the dashboard's Real profit page uses as estimates; once
orders exist, "Money per channel" (PR #52) and "Real fees per order" (PR #54) replace them
with the real numbers from each channel's own report.
